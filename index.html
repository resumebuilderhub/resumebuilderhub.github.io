Frequently asked questions

Is the Resume Builder free to use?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free, needs no sign-up, and runs entirely in your browser.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The tools work in any modern web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. Just open the tool page and use it.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you type is processed in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to a server or stored by this site.

Does the site work on a phone?

Yes. Every page is mobile-friendly, with large buttons and layouts that fit small screens without sideways scrolling.

Can I print my result?

Yes. The builders on this site include a print button that produces a clean page, and you can save it as a PDF from the print dialog.

Why does the tool look different on my device?

The layout adapts to your screen size. On a phone the sections stack in one column, while on a computer they sit side by side.

Who is this site for?

Anyone who needs the tool quickly: students, freelancers, small business owners, teachers, and anyone working from home.

How do I report a problem?

Use the contact page to tell us what went wrong, including which tool you were using and what you expected to happen.
